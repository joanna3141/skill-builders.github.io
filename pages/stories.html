The Jungle

By Caitlyn

In a land far far away there was a jungle that flourished everyday. The sounds of squawking birds and large lion roars filled the atmosphere. Vibrant fruits were filled to the brim with juice for all to eat. At night noises began to soften and all things were not even talking, not even a peep.

Hidden Treasure

By Anonymous

In the middle of the ocean, there was a small island. On that island, legend has it that there was a hidden treasure. One day, a group of pirates came to the island to find the treasure. They searched all over the island but couldn't find any clues. Just when they were about to give up, they found a note inside a bottle on the shore. It warned them to stay away from the island or they would be cursed. The pirates didn't listen. They continued to search until there was a huge storm that swept them away. They were never seen again.
